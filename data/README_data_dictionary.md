# UC19 dummy dataset: service jobs (Sep 2025 – Oct 2026)

All data is synthetic, generated by `gen_data.py` (seed 19; re-running gives identical files).
12,612 service jobs · 7 regions · 18 cities · 65 technicians · 4 brands · 6 product categories · 29 fault codes · 66 parts.

## service_jobs.csv (one row per job)
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| job_id | Job number, J000001… |
| first_visit_date, month | Date of the first technician visit and its month (YYYY-MM) |
| region, city | Turkish geographic region and city of the customer |
| technician_id | Technician who made the first visit (T-001…T-065) |
| brand | Arçelik, Beko, Grundig, Altus |
| category | Washing machine, Refrigerator, Dishwasher, Oven, Dryer, Air conditioner |
| fault_code, fault_description | Diagnosed fault (e.g. WM-E01 Not draining) |
| product_age_years, warranty | Appliance age; "In warranty" if under 3 years |
| parts_needed_count, parts_needed | Part codes the repair needed, separated by "; " (empty = adjustment only) |
| missing_parts | Needed parts that were not on the van at the first visit |
| first_time_fix | 1 = closed on the first visit, 0 = second visit needed |
| second_visit, second_visit_reason | 1/0, and why: Missing part, Wrong diagnosis, Repair not successful, Customer not available |
| second_visit_date, days_to_resolve | Date of the second visit; days from first to second visit (0 if fixed first time) |

## job_parts.csv (one row per part needed in a job)
job_id, part_code, part_name, qty, on_van_first_visit (Y/N).

## Reference tables
- `fault_codes.csv`: fault_code, category, fault_description
- `parts_catalogue.csv`: part_code, part_name, assumed_van_stock_rate (share of vans assumed to carry it)
- `technicians.csv`: technician_id, region, city, tenure_years

## Assumptions used to generate the data
- Volume ~800–1,200 jobs a month; air conditioners peak in June–August, refrigerators in summer, ovens in winter.
- Each fault code has a fixed list of possible parts, each needed with its own probability (e.g. WM-E01: drain pump 70%, pump filter 25%, drain hose 10%).
- Whether a needed part is on the van depends on the part (cheap, common parts ~90%; boards, compressors, bearings, oven glass 22–45%), the region (Marmara/Ege better stocked; Karadeniz, Güneydoğu and Doğu Anadolu worse), the technician, and a small improvement over the year.
- If any needed part is missing, the job needs a second visit for "Missing part"; otherwise small fixed chances of wrong diagnosis, failed repair or customer not at home.
- Lead time to the second visit: 2–7 days for a missing part, +0–4 days in eastern regions, +3 days for rarely stocked parts; 1–4 days for other reasons.
- Every job has one first visit and at most one second visit (no third visits).
